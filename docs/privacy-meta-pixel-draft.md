## Advertising measurement (Meta Pixel)

With your permission, we use the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc., on our public website pages to measure how our Facebook and Instagram ads perform and to show our vehicles to people who may be interested.

**It is off by default.** The Meta Pixel loads only after you choose **Accept** in our cookie banner. If you choose Decline, or make no choice, it does not load and no information is sent to Meta through it.

**What it collects when you accept:** the pages you view on our site, technical information your browser sends (such as IP address, browser type and device information), and cookies or similar identifiers that Meta sets or reads (such as `_fbp`). Meta may combine this with information it has about you if you use Facebook or Instagram.

**What we do not send:** we do not send the contents of our test-drive request form, bug reports, or any information you type into our forms through the Meta Pixel.

**Your choices:** you can change your choice at any time using **Privacy choices** at the bottom of our pages. If you withdraw consent, the page reloads and the Pixel stops loading. You can also manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings and learn more in Meta's Privacy Policy (https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

**Admin areas:** the Meta Pixel never loads on our staff administration or security pages.
